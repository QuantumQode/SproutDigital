## Design Specificity Verdict

**Start here.** Does the result feel authored for this product, or category-interchangeable?

**LLM assessment**: Authored, not templated. The "Living Greenhouse" system is genuinely followed through — a single hue-150 OKLCH palette, `light-dark()` tokens for a real dark mode (not a bolted-on reskin), pure-CSS browser mockups instead of stock photography, a named-area bento grid, and consistent pill/rounding rules. The rotating hero word, mousemove tilt on the hero mockup, and URL-param handoff from pricing/work cards into the contact form (`plan=Growth`, `project=NinjaPlumbers`) are bespoke craft details a generic template wouldn't bother with. Where it slips toward category-interchangeable: the overall page shape (hero → logo marquee → bento stats → dark "results dashboard" → testimonials → portfolio → CTA) is now a very common indie-SaaS/agency pattern, and stacking that many simultaneous proof mechanisms reads as "confident startup template" more than "solo operator being honest about being new."

**Deterministic scan**: The detector ran clean on `pricing.html` and `work.html` (0 findings each). Across `index.html`, `services.html`, and `contact.html` it found 8 total issues, all advisory-severity (exit code 2, but nothing blocking): 6 undocumented literal colors — ad-hoc Google-blue (`oklch(0.65 0.17 250)`) and Meta-purple (`oklch(0.65 0.2 300)`) chip-dot colors used for the paid-ads channel chips, plus two colors close to but not matching existing tokens — none registered in `DESIGN.md`'s palette; and em-dash overuse in body copy (10 in `index.html`, 10 in `contact.html`, filtered for repeated nav/footer boilerplate). No false positives — every flagged color was checked against the documented palette and genuinely isn't in it.

**Visual overlays**: Not available this run — no browser automation tool (claude-in-chrome / Playwright / Puppeteer) was connected in this session, and the project has no dev server. Browser visualization, live-server startup, and detect.js injection were all skipped cleanly rather than attempted and failed; there is no user-visible overlay to check in a `[Human]` tab this time.

## Overall Impression

The craft is real — this doesn't read as a templated agency site, and the "Living Greenhouse" identity is followed with discipline down to dark-mode tokens and pure-CSS mockups. But the site is currently working against its own stated positioning: it stacks four separate proof mechanisms (logo marquee, bento stats, results dashboard, testimonials) around the *same* three fictional identities with inconsistent numbers, which risks reading as recycled rather than credible once a skeptical small-business buyer looks twice — exactly the audience PRODUCT.md says this has to win over. And structurally, the single biggest problem isn't a design opinion at all: the contact form's Web3Forms key is still the literal placeholder string, so right now every "Get a free audit" CTA on the site — there are 9 of them — terminates in a submission that silently fails.

## What's Working

1. **Browser-mock illustrations instead of stock photography** — the pure-CSS browser frames in the hero and work cards literally illustrate "a website that grows," tying visual identity directly to the product being sold.
2. **Contact-page reassurance architecture** — the "what happens next" 3-point list, honeypot field, inline validation, and success-state swap are well-calibrated for a skeptical small-business buyer's biggest hesitation (will anyone actually reply?).
3. **URL-param context carryover** — picking a plan on `pricing.html` or a project on `work.html` pre-fills the contact form. A small detail most agency-template sites skip, and it directly reduces friction at the moment of highest intent.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Contact form cannot deliver submissions**
- **Why it matters**: `pages/contact.html:97` still holds `value="YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY"`. Every CTA on the site funnels here — the site cannot capture a single real lead until this is fixed. This matches the gap already flagged in PRODUCT.md's Capabilities and Constraints.
- **Fix**: Swap in a real Web3Forms access key before any real traffic reaches the site.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The same fictional client is reused across 4 proof surfaces with inconsistent numbers**
- **Why it matters**: NinjaPlumbers / James T. appears in the hero float card, the bento quote, the testimonials grid, and the work-portfolio card — each with a different stat attached. This undermines exactly the credibility a client-less solo agency most needs, and a careful reader will notice the repetition faster than they'd notice an honest "we're new" framing.
- **Fix**: Diversify which placeholder identity appears in which section, or consolidate proof into fewer, more honest placeholder moments consistent with PRODUCT.md's instruction not to fabricate further beyond what's already there.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Contact form's two-column rows don't collapse on mobile**
- **Why it matters**: `.form-row { grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr }` (`css/styles.css:1091`) has no override in any of the file's three `@media` blocks. Name/Email and Website/Plan fields stay side-by-side on a 375px phone, cramping input at the single highest-stakes conversion moment, for an audience (Casey, mobile) very likely to be on a phone.
- **Fix**: Add `.form-row { grid-template-columns: 1fr; }` under the existing 640px or 860px breakpoint.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Homepage stacks 9 competing proof/motion sections**
- **Why it matters**: Logo marquee, bento counters, a dark "results dashboard" (funnel + ticker + a "Live" pulse on static demo data), testimonials, work teaser, and a stat-laden CTA box all run concurrently. The same 4.6x ROAS figure is asserted twice (bento tile and again in the ads dashboard). Nothing signals which single number a visitor should walk away remembering, and the volume reads as over-engineered for a one-person shop — in tension with PRODUCT.md's "one operator, full accountability" positioning.
- **Fix**: Cut the logo marquee (the weakest and most obviously synthetic proof surface) and stop restating the same ROAS number twice.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

**[P3] Pricing's ad-spend disclaimer is buried**
- **Why it matters**: "Ad spend is paid directly to Google/Meta on top of your plan" sits as small muted text below the CTA buttons rather than near each price, so budget-sensitive buyers may miss the true total cost until later in the conversation.
- **Fix**: Move the caveat inline, next to each recurring plan's price.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`
