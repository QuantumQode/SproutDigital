Questions

Frequently asked.

How much should I budget for Google or Meta ads?+
Most clients start between £200–£1,200/month in ad spend, depending on the size of the business. We'll recommend a realistic starting budget in your free audit based on your industry and goals, then scale it up only once the numbers prove it's paying for itself.
How quickly will I see results from paid ads?+
Unlike SEO, paid ads start driving clicks the day they go live. Expect the first leads within the first week or two, with performance improving over the first 90 days as we optimize targeting, ads, and landing pages.
Do I need a new website before running ads?+
Not always — but ads are only as good as the page they land on. If your current site loads slowly or doesn't convert, we'll usually recommend fixing the landing page first so your ad budget isn't wasted.
Am I locked into a contract?+
Only briefly. Every Launch build includes Foundation (hosting & care), which has a 6-month minimum from launch and then rolls monthly. Growth and Scale include Foundation, so time on them counts toward those 6 months. Beyond that, every plan is rolling monthly and you can step down or cancel anytime. Your website, ad accounts, and data are always yours to keep.
How do you report on performance?+
You get a plain-English monthly report: what we spent, what it earned, cost per lead, and what we're changing next month. No vanity metrics, no jargon.
